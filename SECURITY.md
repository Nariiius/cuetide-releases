# Security and privacy contact

Found a vulnerability, sensitive bug or have a privacy/deletion request? Email **[nariushere@gmail.com](mailto:nariushere@gmail.com)** privately. **Do not open an issue.** Narius operates Cuetide from Victoria, Australia.

Include the Cuetide/macOS version, a short description and the minimum useful reproduction or request. Do not send keys, passwords, full libraries, identity documents or unredacted logs. If a credential was exposed, revoke or rotate it with its provider.

Issues and attachments are public and may be indexed or copied. Removing a post cannot guarantee removal of other copies. Email is not a promise of end-to-end encryption or a guaranteed response time.

Privacy requests cover information Narius controls, such as support correspondence—not remote erasure of your Mac or records held by GitHub, Apple or other providers. For local removal, follow the [README](README.md#remove-cuetide); **never delete the whole `~/Library/Application Support/Music/` folder**. Local deletion does not undo Music playlist changes.
