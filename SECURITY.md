# Security and private privacy requests

Contact **Narius** privately at **nariushere@gmail.com** for vulnerabilities, sensitive bug reports, privacy questions or deletion requests. Narius operates Cuetide from Victoria, Australia.

Do **not** open an issue for these reports. Repository access is currently private, but issues and attachments may become public, indexed or copied when the repository becomes public. Email is a private contact channel, not a promise of end-to-end encryption or a guaranteed response time.

## Report a vulnerability or sensitive bug

Start with the Cuetide version, macOS version, a short description and the minimum steps needed to explain the problem. For a vulnerability, describe the potential impact privately. Do not send API keys, account credentials, full library exports, unredacted logs or unrelated personal information. Redact screenshots and listening information. If more evidence is needed, we can discuss a minimal redacted example.

If you have already exposed a credential, revoke or rotate it with its provider. Edit or remove the public content where possible and use GitHub's support/privacy channels for platform-held information. Removing a post cannot guarantee removal of indexed, cached or third-party copies.

## Privacy and deletion requests

Describe the information you shared with Narius and what you want corrected or deleted. Do not send identity documents or additional sensitive data merely to make a request. If the scope is unclear, we may ask for limited clarification.

Cuetide does not provide a developer-hosted account or telemetry database. Information you voluntarily email or post to GitHub is separate from local app data and provider lookups. Requests can address information under Narius's control; GitHub, Apple and metadata/lyrics providers handle their own data under their policies. We cannot promise universal erasure or removal of third-party backups and copies.

For local data removal, ask for the precise Cuetide paths if unsure. **Never delete the whole `~/Library/Application Support/Music/` directory**: it is shared and may contain unrelated data. Local deletion does not undo changes made to Music playlists or delete information held by providers.

GitHub's own handling is described in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
